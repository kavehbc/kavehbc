I build intelligent systems where **research, engineering, and human behavior** meet. My work spans machine learning, deep learning, recommender systems, reinforcement learning, generative AI, and affective computing, always guided by a simple principle: choose methods thoughtfully, challenge assumptions, and measure what truly works.

Today, I lead AI/ML engineering at [Kinship Partners](https://kinship.com/) and teach AI and computer science at [LaSalle College Montreal](https://collegelasalle.com/).

> **“Do what you can. Dare to try what you cannot yet.”**

I focus on **production ML, deep learning, LLMs, recommender systems, reinforcement learning, optimization, affective computing, and human-computer interaction**.
